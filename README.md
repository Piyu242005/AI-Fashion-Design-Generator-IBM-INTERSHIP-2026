## 📌 Introduction

This repository is a comprehensive documentation of my **IBM SkillsBuild Artificial Intelligence Internship**, conducted by **Edunet Foundation**. It serves as a living portfolio of everything I learned, built, and explored throughout the program — from foundational AI concepts to hands-on Generative AI experiments.

Whether you're a fellow learner, a recruiter, or an AI enthusiast, this repository reflects real growth through structured learning, practical labs, and project-based application.

> 💡 *"The goal is not to build AI. The goal is to understand it well enough to build what matters."*
